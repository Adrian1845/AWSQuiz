## Explanation

That's right! Don't give more permissions than the user needs.

## Why the other options are incorrect

- **Grant most privilege:** It's a bad practice to grant maximum privileges to all users.
- **Grant more permissions if your employee asks you to:** You shouldn't grant permissions to your employees unless it's relevant to their work.
- **Restrict root account permissions:** You can not restrict root account permissions.
