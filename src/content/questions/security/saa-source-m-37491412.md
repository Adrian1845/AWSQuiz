## Explanation

IAM Credentials report lists all your AWS Account's IAM Users and the status of their various credentials.

## Why the other options are incorrect

- **IAM Root Account Manager:** There's no such AWS service.
- **IAM Services Report:** There's no such AWS service.
- **IAM Security Advisor:** There's no such AWS service.
