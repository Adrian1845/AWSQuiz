## Explanation

Amazon Translate is a neural machine translation service that delivers fast, high-quality, and affordable language translation.

## Why the other options are incorrect

- **Transcribe:** Amazon Transcribe is an AWS service that makes it easy for customers to convert speech-to-text.
- **Polly:** Amazon Polly is a service that turns text into lifelike speech.
- **WordTranslator:** This is a distractor. This service does not exist.
